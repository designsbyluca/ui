# FeatureStack

Service sections that stick to the top and slide over each other on scroll; the covered text fades and blurs.

**Pro**

Each item: a `Kicker`, two short sentences as `feature-title`, one to three short paragraphs, a default and an outline button, and a 9:10 image on the right . Two to four items. `top` = height of a floating NavBar. No sticking below 900px.

**Motion:** follows scroll (`dur-scrub`). The previous section fades to 15 % and blurs to 4px as the next one arrives.
