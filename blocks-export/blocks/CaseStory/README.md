# CaseStory

A full-screen story of a project: slides with a progress bar, tap left or right to move.

**Pro** `inline` renders it in the page as 16:9. Slides take `title`, `text`, `caption` (serif capitals) and `media`. Five seconds per slide by default; arrow keys and Escape work.

**Motion:** fades in over `dur-overlay` after 200ms; the bar fills linearly.
